## Den's Take

The collection of industry snippets suggests a generalized fear of AI failure, but the focus remains too high-level. What these reports gloss over is the operational gap between theoretical model fragility and the reality of deployed systems. A shift in vulnerabilities noted in AI-Hardened Network Intrusion Detection Systems, for instance, implies that current defensive models are not just being bypassed by clever prompts, but by subtle changes in the data or execution environment. If we accept the premise that AI infrastructure growth increases fragility, then the immediate concern isn't just "misalignment"; it's the verifiable integrity of the data pipelines feeding these systems. Relying on high-level safety warnings ignores the practical need to audit the entire consumption chain, which is where the most immediate exploits will manifest.
