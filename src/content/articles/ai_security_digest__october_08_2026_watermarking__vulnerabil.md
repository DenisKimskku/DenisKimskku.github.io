## Den's Take

The framing around watermarking as a primary solution for provenance feels incomplete. While formalizing detection signals, as described in the paper on Sok, addresses *detection*, it entirely sidesteps the systemic issue of *generation control*. If the primary risk in generative AI is the ability of an attacker to use a model's output for malicious purposes—a functional compromise—then simply tagging the output doesn't prevent the misuse. For instance, if a model is used to generate highly convincing phishing content, the watermark only tells you *that* it was generated by AI, not *how* the generated text was weaponized. We should be focusing less on forensic tagging and more on architectural constraints that restrict the *utility* of the generated output in adversarial contexts.
